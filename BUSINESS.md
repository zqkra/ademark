# ADEMark funding

Version: v1.0, 2026-10-19 (first results published 2026-11-12).

ADEMark sells nothing to vendors. It accepts donations to cover its costs. No donation can change a result.

## 1. Donations

- **Who can donate:** anyone.
- **Where:** one "Donate" link to Ko-fi in the site footer, shown once the Ko-fi page exists (v1.0.0 has none). It
  does not appear on charts, result pages or ADE cards.
- **Supporters page:** a donor can show a name, a photo and a link, or stay anonymous. Anonymous donations show
  as a monthly count and total.

## 2. What a donation never buys

No donation, payment, discount or favor buys any of these:

1. Inclusion in the field, or exclusion of any ADE.
2. Position, grade, tier or a place in any chart.
3. The choice of tasks, prompts, tests, models or settings.
4. The date or timing of a publication, or a delay of one.
5. A preview of results before they are public.
6. The removal or edit of a published result. Only method errors are fixed, in the public corrections log.

## 3. Free for every ADE

- Inclusion when it meets the inclusion rule in [METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md) §2.
- A scheduled re-run every 60 days on its newest stable release.
- The right to reply next to its row, unedited.
- The ADEMark badge while its results are current. The badge shows the date of the result.
- Its improvement report (what to fix first, from the data).

## 4. Costs

The supporters page shows what the money pays for, and a yearly report of income and spending:

| Cost | Amount |
|---|---|
| Claude Max 5x, dedicated benchmark account | 100 USD per month |
| ChatGPT Plus, dedicated benchmark account | 20 USD per month |
| Domain (ademark.dev) and hosting (Cloudflare) | domain yearly fee; hosting on the free plan |
| Model API usage outside the plans (for example Capy with an API key) | per run, published |
